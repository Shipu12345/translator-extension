German → English Translator

Translation runs on your computer with Chrome's built-in translator. It's free, needs no account, and nothing leaves your machine.

How to use

  1. Open a German web page.
  2. Click the extension icon, or press Alt+T (⌥+T on Mac). English appears under each German paragraph as you scroll. Click again to turn it off.
  3. First time on a site: a small box appears at the top right. Click Enable translation once. Chrome requires one click on each new site, and the first time ever it also downloads the language model (about 100 MB).

More

  • Translation stays on for that tab while you browse, and never starts by itself in other tabs.
  • Translate a selection: select text, then right-click → Translate selection to English, or press Alt+Shift+T.
  • Change shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • Tip: pin the extension via the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar.
